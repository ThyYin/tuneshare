**Tunetopia** is a Discord bot for **saving and sharing your favourite songs and albums**.

Type in a song or album name, or paste a Spotify / YT Music / SoundCloud track link - boom! Just like that, Tunetopia keeps your personal list of tracks (and albums) you and your pals can browse, filter, and share; THE scrumptious **music diary** for your server! 🥞🎶

It does **not** join voice channels or play audio; it saves your taste.

## What you can do

- **Search songs by name** to add into your music diary 🔍
- **Accept and save songs** from Spotify / YT Music / SoundCloud links 🔗
- **Browse your song list** with pages + an artist filter 📜
- **Peek at someone else's favs** 👀
- **Favourite albums too**, then browse them the same way 💿
- **Look up song info** and **album tracklists** 💽
- Look up **artist** info and their **album catalogues** 👤
- **Peek at a user's (incl. yourself) top artists**, ranked by how many of their songs are in a fav list 🎖️



## Quick start (general workflow)

1. `/fav [song_name]` or paste a Spotify / YT Music / SoundCloud track link
2. Pick your desired song if you searched by name (bot shows the **top 5 search results**). Hit the red **Cancel** button to abort action.
3. `/favs` to view your song list, or `/favs @someone` to peek at theirs
4. `/favab [album_name_or_link]` to save an album, `/favsab` to see your favourited album list, or `/favsab @someone` to peek at theirs.
5. `/song [song_name_or_link]`, `/album [album_name_or_link]`, or `/artist [artist_name]` for the lore
6. `/catalog [artist_name]` — pick the artist, browse albums, tap one for the tracklist, then **Back**



## Commands

Same commands work with the `t!` prefix, e.g. `t!fav`, `t!help`.

`t!view` / `/view` is gone — use `t!favs @someone` / `/favs @someone` instead.


| Command       | What it does                                                                                                             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/fav`        | **Save a song by name or link.** Name search shows numbered picks. Hit Cancel to abort.                                  |
| `/favs`       | **Your favourited songs**, or tag someone to peek at theirs (pages + artist filter)                                      |
| `/unfav`      | **Remove a song from ur fav list.** Browse your list (pages + artist filter), or search by title. Hit Cancel to abort.   |
| `/favab`      | **Save an album by name or link.** Name search shows top 5 results. A Spotify album, Spotify playlist, Deezer, or SoundCloud link saves it directly. Hit Cancel to abort. |
| `/favsab`     | Your **favourite albums**, or tag someone to peek at theirs (pages + artist filter)                                      |
| `/unfavab`    | **Remove an album from ur fav list.** Browse your list (pages + artist filter), or search by title. Hit Cancel to abort. |
| `/song`       | Cover, title, artist, album, year, and stats                                                                             |
| `/album`      | Album name or link. A link opens the **tracklist**. A name shows the top 5, then the tracks                              |
| `/artist`     | Type artist name, pick from the top 5 results, then view artist's **portrait**, **genre**, and **years**                 |
| `/catalog`    | Type artist name, pick from the top 5 results, then browse **album tracks produced by the artist**                       |
| `/topartists` | Artists ranked by **how many songs** you (or someone else) have favourited                                               |
| `/help`       | Shows detailed bot **commands**                                                                                          |
| `/ping`       | Checks whether the bot is **online** (Annie are you okay?)                                                               |




## 💡Good to know

- **Track links for** `/fav` — Spotify / YT Music / SoundCloud links. Album and playlist links aren't saved there. Use `/favab` with an album link or name instead. 🔗
- **Album links for** `/favab` and `/album` — Spotify, YouTube Music, Deezer, and SoundCloud. YouTube Music album links look like `music.youtube.com/playlist?list=OLAK5uy_...`. 💿
- **No duplicates** — you can't save the exact same song link or album twice. 
- **Pickers are yours** — only you can press the numbered buttons / Cancel on a search you started. 😉
- **Not a music player** — no voice channel, no queue, no playback. You'll have to ask Jockie Music for that. 📻
- Built for hanging out in servers and sharing your wholesome music tastes with your pals. ✨
